The doctor's room

Doctors speak. ClinicOS writes — and helps them think.

No keyboard between the doctor and the patient. Just talk — the record fills itself, and a quiet second opinion is right there when it helps.

  • ● Live now
    Voice to note. Tap the mic and speak. The consultation note writes itself, in the doctor's own words — no typing, nothing lost, eyes on the patient.
  • In development
    A clinical co-pilot. As symptoms and history go in, ClinicOS Assist suggests the likely conditions, flags danger signs, and recommends what to check next — cross-checked against the patient's allergies. A second opinion in the room. The doctor always decides.

In development · with founding clinics

Your monthly Ministry report — filed for you.

Every clinic owes the Ministry of Health a monthly report. Miss the deadline and there are penalties. ClinicOS already has every diagnosis and every visit — so it does the report for you.

  • It compiles itself. Your top-10 diseases and monthly figures, built straight from the records you already entered — no re-typing into a government portal.
  • It files itself. Submitted directly to the national platform, dhis2.moh.gov.et — automatically each month, or reviewed and sent in one click.
  • Deadlines stop being your problem. No month-end scramble, no penalty risk, no data-entry evening.

Questions

The things owners ask us first.

I already have a system. Why switch? +

If your current system tells you exactly how much came in today, what's leaking, and what's happening while you're away — keep it. Most don't. ClinicOS is built for that visibility, in ETB, around your real flow. Try it on a few clinics; the numbers make the case.

Is my patient data safe? +

Each clinic's data is isolated and private, and staff only see what their role needs. It's your clinic's data — we don't sell it, share it, or mix it with anyone else's.

What if the internet goes down? +

ClinicOS runs on a server you control, so it's resilient to patchy connections. We'll walk you through the setup that fits your clinic during onboarding.

Do I need expensive hardware? +

No. It runs on one modest server and ordinary computers or tablets your staff already use.

Will my staff be able to use it? +

Yes — it follows the way your clinic already works, so it's familiar on day one. And we train your team as part of setup.

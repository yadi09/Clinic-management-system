Why this matters

A product clinics genuinely need — and a voice they already trust.

ClinicOS is the clinic operating system built for Ethiopia.

Across the country, private clinics still run on paper and spreadsheets — money leaks through untracked consultations, pharmacy and lab; reception runs on memory; and owners fly blind the moment they leave the building. ClinicOS fixes that — reception to the last birr, in ETB, on hardware they already have, shaped to how Ethiopian clinics actually work.

Doctors dictate notes by voice, an AI co-pilot supports diagnosis, and the clinic's monthly Ministry (DHIS2) report is filed automatically — capabilities no foreign system offers here.

Why we're inviting you

You already have what money can't buy: the trust and attention of the exact people who need this — clinic owners, doctors, and health leaders. When you recommend ClinicOS, you're not selling software; you're helping the clinics who follow you run better, serve more patients, and stop losing money. We reward you generously for it.

The home dashboard, redesigned

The home page is the first thing every person sees when they sign in. Today it shows numbers, but several of them are wrong, two contradict each other, and none of them says what to do next. This page shows what is wrong, the new design you can click through, the tools it is built with, and the plan. Nothing is built until you approve it.

What is wrong today

Taken from your screenshot of Cure Dental this morning and from reading the code behind each number.

1"Owed to you" is missing half the money

It says 4,500 ETB. Right above it, "6 visits treated and never billed" is another 4,800 ETB the patients also owe. The real figure is 9,300 ETB, which is what the Finance page shows. Two pages, two answers.

One "Owed to you" number, split into "at the cashier" and "not sent yet".

2"Seen today: 0" in a dental clinic, always

It counts closed doctor's notes. A dental visit is finished at the chairside, which never closes a note, so it stays at 0 however many patients you treat.

Counts patients who finished their visit today, the way the reception screen already does.

3"Booked today: 0" with 2 patients in the chair

It only reads the appointments book. Dental follow-up visits are booked inside the treatment, so they are never counted, and neither are walk-ins.

Today's bookings include treatment visits, with walk-ins named separately.

4"97% vs yesterday" hides a drop

600 ETB against 18,600 yesterday reads like good news. It also compares this morning against all of yesterday, which is unfair before noon every day.

"700 ETB more than yesterday at this time", in green or red, in words.

5A chart that says almost nothing

Fourteen grey slivers with no amounts, no days, no today marker. "19,200 ETB" at the top looks like it contradicts "600 today" because it doesn't say it is the 14-day total.

A real chart: amounts, days, a highlighted today, a tap-to-read tooltip, 7, 30 or 90 days.

6If the page fails to load, it says "all clear"

A network error shows the green "Nothing needs you" card. An owner could believe there is no unbilled treatment when the page simply didn't load.

A clear "Couldn't load, try again" in place of each section.

7It ignores the branch you are in

A clinic with two branches sees both branches' money and queues mixed together, whichever branch is selected at the top.

Every number follows the active branch; owners can pick "All branches".

8The same page for everyone

A doctor gets clinic totals instead of "your patients". A pharmacist or lab technician gets an empty page with one button. A cashier sees "Owed to you" but cannot open Finance.

The page shapes itself to the person: owner, doctor, reception, cashier.

9Smaller things

"Overdue 60 days" opens a list filtered to 90+, hiding some of them. The greeting uses the computer's clock, not Addis. "Booked ahead" shows "0 ETB" in clinics that are not dental. Labels are shouted in capitals.

Each fixed in the same pass.

The new dashboard

Sample numbers, for a dental clinic a month after it starts. Switch the person signed in, hover or tap the charts, and change the chart's range. The phone version further down is the same page, rearranged.

On a phone

The same page, not a cut-down one. Every section stacks in the order of what you act on first: what needs you, who is in the clinic, the money, then the charts.

  • Today's flow becomes a 2 × 2 grid with the progress bar under it.
  • Money tiles go two per row; the numbers shrink to fit, never wrap.
  • Charts keep their tooltip: tap a bar to read it, tap elsewhere to close.
  • Charts load after the numbers. On a slow connection the tiles appear first and the charts fill in a moment later, so the page never waits for a chart.
  • No sideways scrolling at 360 px, the smallest phones in use.

How each part reads

1. Needs you

  • At most three, the costliest first, each with one button that does the job: "Send to cashier", "Rebook", "Review".
  • When there is nothing, one calm green line. When the page fails, it says so instead.

2. Today

  • The patient journey left to right: booked → waiting → in the chair → done. The longest wait is named, so a patient forgotten in the waiting room gets noticed.
  • "Still to come" lists who is booked and not here yet, with walk-ins marked.
  • Updates by itself every 30 seconds; the green dot says it is live.

3. Money

  • Collected today against yesterday at the same hour, in words and colour.
  • Owed to you is one number, the same as Finance, split into at the cashier and not sent yet.
  • Each tile has a 14-day line so the trend is visible at a glance, and opens the page behind it.

4. Charts

  • Money in: one bar a day, today highlighted, amounts on the axis, a tooltip with the day and number of payments.
  • Where it came from: treatment, consultation, X-ray and services, pharmacy; and how it was paid: cash, telebirr, bank.
  • Who owes you by how long, with the five largest by name. The week ahead shows booked visits per day.

Tools

Your app has no chart or animation library today; the current charts are drawn by hand. These were compared on size, upkeep and fit with the app's existing parts.

The plan

Phase 1Right numbers
  • Fix every number listed above on the server: one "owed", seen today, booked today, branch, Addis clock, the 60-day link.
  • Add what the new parts need: 90 days of daily takings, takings by source and by payment method, owed by age with the top five, the week ahead, who is still to come today, the longest wait.
  • Tested against a copy of real data so the dashboard and Finance agree to the birr.
Phase 2New owner page
  • Needs you, Today, Money tiles with trend lines, the four charts, error and empty states.
  • Charts in their own bundle, loaded after the numbers. Phone, tablet and desktop checked in a real browser, as with the visit card.
Phase 3Every role
  • Doctor: your patients waiting, in your chair, done; your missed visits to rebook.
  • Reception: the flow, who is still to come, the week's bookings. Cashier: today's takings by method, what is waiting at the till, close the day.
  • Pharmacy and lab go straight to their own dashboards.
LaterOptional
  • Today's flow updating the instant a patient checks in (the app already has a live queue feed).
  • Dark mode for the whole app. The colours are ready, the switch is not.

To decide before building

"Owed to you": one number or two?

Suggested: one number (9,300), split underneath into "at the cashier" and "not sent yet", matching the Finance page exactly.

What should "Collected today" compare against?

Suggested: yesterday at the same hour. Alternative: the same weekday last week, which is fairer for clinics with very different days, but harder to grasp.

Which range should the money chart open on?

Suggested: 30 days, with 7 and 90 one tap away. The page remembers the last choice.

Pharmacy and lab staff: their own dashboard, or this page?

Suggested: their own dashboard, which already exists for pharmacy; lab gets its worklist.

Build all three phases, or phases 1 and 2 first?

Suggested: 1 and 2 first, deploy, let Cure Dental use it for a few days, then the role views.

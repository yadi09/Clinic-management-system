Patient portal — design preview

A preview with made-up data (patient Meron, card MRN-000123). Nothing here is live. Use the phone on the left like a patient would.

For this demo: sign-in is the card number only, as you asked. Any number works here. The real build will check it against the clinic’s patients — and we will add the safer code later.

Sign in

  • One field: the card number. Nothing to remember, no password.
  • The page carries the clinic’s logo and name: it feels like the clinic’s own app.
  • The clinic’s card and receipts print a QR to this page.

Where each screen’s data comes from ClinicOS

  • Home: next appointment or visit, the current treatment, the balance, and an optional message from the clinic.
  • Treatment: the clinic’s treatment plans and their visits, in plain words.
  • Payments: the same money figures the cashier sees (paid, owed, not yet billed), receipts, the payment plan, and Pay from home.
  • My teeth: the live tooth chart — 2D, and the 3D model. Everything is read-only.

Pay from home New

  • What can be paid: anything already billed and unpaid, and the next visit paid ahead. Work that is done but not billed yet is shown greyed out — the patient can’t pay a bill that does not exist.
  • How: the patient picks the items, sees the clinic’s own Telebirr / bank account (from Payment rails) and a reference code, pays in their banking app, then types the transaction number from the SMS.
  • Checking: ClinicOS verifies that number with the bank (Verify.ET), the same way the cashier’s check works. The patient sees three honest states: Submitted → Checking → Confirmed, or Not found with what to do next.
  • Never auto-trusted: a payment becomes a receipt only when verification says it matches the amount and the clinic’s account. If the check is unsure, it stays “waiting” and the cashier sees it to confirm — money is never marked paid on the patient’s word.
  • Same books: a confirmed payment lands on the same charges and the day’s cashier report as a payment at the desk, marked “paid from home”. A transaction number can be used once.
  • Limitsno refunds from the portal (the clinic handles those); the bank sometimes takes minutes to show a payment, so “checking” can last a little; real clinics need Verify.ET switched on.

Safeguards in the real build

  • The patient can only ever see their own record; the server takes the patient from the sign-in, never from the page address.
  • Read-only. Pages are hidden from search engines. Every sign-in is recorded for the clinic.
  • Wrong tries are limited, and every failure gets the same message.
  • Latera second step (a code on the card, or by SMS) before real patients use it.

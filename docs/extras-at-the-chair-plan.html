Extras at the chair: send to the cashier, pay, then continue

The "Also done at this visit" section: what it does today, why the Send to cashier button is missing in your screenshot, whether the patient should pay before the procedure continues, and a cleaner design for the whole section. Nothing is built yet: this is for your review.

My answer in short. Yes, the patient should normally pay before an extra procedure goes ahead, and the screen should make that the obvious path. I would do it as a visible sequence (Not billed, At cashier, Paid) with "Treat now, pay later" always available for urgent cases, exactly as the visit fee works today, rather than a hard lock. The button you asked for already exists in the code; it is not showing on this form because that form never passes it the permission. That part is a small fix.

1. What happens today

  • An extra is a small visit of its own. Adding "X-ray, 2,000 ETB" creates a row attached to the visit. It is owed from that moment ("Owed now" in your screenshot).
  • A "Send to cashier" button exists (it raises the bill on the cashier's list under the name "Root canal, x-ray at visit 3"). It shows on the visit screen, but not on the form in your screenshot, because that form does not tell the section the user may bill. So you see the price and "Owed now" with no way to act. That is the gap you noticed.
  • Nothing stops or warns the dentist from carrying on or finishing the visit while the extra is unpaid, and the dentist cannot see when the patient has paid without leaving and returning.
  • Zero price already means "included in the course", and an unbilled extra can be removed with a confirmation. Both stay.

2. Should the patient pay before the procedure continues?

I think yes, as the normal path, for three reasons: it is how the clinic already treats the visit fee and the lab and pharmacy (pay, then it opens); it avoids the awkward "you owe 2,000 for the X-ray we just took" after the fact; and extras are exactly the things patients are surprised by.

But I would not make it a hard lock, for the same reason the visit fee is not one:

SituationWhat the screen does
Normal: the dentist decides an X-ray is neededAdd it, press Send to cashier. The patient pays; the chair screen turns green by itself. The dentist continues.
Pain, bleeding, an emergency, a known patient who settles monthlyTreat now, pay later (with a reason). It stays owed and stays on the cashier's list; it is not written off.
A small clinic where the dentist also takes the moneyCollect here opens the same payment step reception uses (method, reference, bank check) without leaving the chair.
Patient says no to the X-rayRemove it (confirmation), nothing is owed.
The dentist tries to finish the visit with an extra unpaidA gentle stop: "X-ray 2,000 ETB has not been paid." with Send to cashier / Treat now, pay later / Finish anyway.

Why not a hard lock: the system cannot stop a hand in a mouth, and an extra is recorded as it happens. A lock that is easy to get round teaches staff to ignore it. A clear sequence, a visible "waiting for payment" and a recorded reason when it is skipped give the clinic the control it actually needs.

5. How it is built

  • Mostly the front end. Billing an extra already goes through the same endpoint as a visit, pay later is already stored per row, and the cashier already lists it. The section is redrawn, the button gets its permission, and the page re-checks while something is at the cashier (every few seconds, only while an extra is waiting).
  • One small server addition: the cashier line text ("Extra at the chair: X-ray, visit 3") and a test that proves an extra added in the chair can be billed, paid and seen as paid on the visit.
  • Permissions unchanged: raising the bill is the existing "charge" permission (the dentist has it); taking the money stays with the cashier unless the clinic has given the dentist the collect permission.
  • Everything stays on the clinic's money definition: an extra counts in "owed", reports and the day close exactly as it does now.

8. Decisions for you

My recommendation is first in each line.

  1. Pay before continuing: sequence with a visible stop, or a hard lock? Recommend: sequence (a gentle stop on finishing; "Treat now, pay later" with a reason). A hard lock cannot be enforced at the chair and gets worked around.
  2. Send to cashier automatically when an extra is added? Recommend: no. Adding and billing stay two deliberate steps, so a mistaken tap does not put a bill in front of the cashier. "Send all" makes it one press when you want it.
  3. Who may say "treat now, pay later" for an extra? Recommend: the treating dentist, with the reason recorded (same as the visit fee today). The alternative is managers only.
  4. Which extras appear as quick-add chips? Recommend: the clinic's five most-used, chosen automatically from what has actually been added.
  5. Should the dentist be able to collect money at the chair? Recommend: only where the clinic has given that person the collect permission; the button is simply absent otherwise.
  6. Start with phase 1 (button and stages) now? Recommend: yes, it fixes the gap you hit today.

Reception: what to improve

A read-through of the reception page, the server behind it and the tests around it (6 October 2026), turned into a list of things worth changing, most valuable first. It starts with the feature you described, because that one is the biggest, and then everything else I found.

In short. The page is already built around the right idea: the day's list first, search second, and one panel for the person in front of you. What it lacks is help with the money conversation (it tells the desk a patient owes, but cannot itemise it or take it in the same payment), a search that puts the right person first, and speed for the common case (the same two dropdowns chosen again and again). Seven known problems on the tracker also sit on this page and should be fixed together with it.

1. Your example: old unpaid bills, at the desk

What the page does today

  • A patient with earlier unpaid bills shows a red or amber tag in the list ("Owes 2,000 ETB · 38d") and a box in the panel: "Owes ETB 2,000.00 from earlier visits · oldest 38 days" with a See it button.
  • See it sends the receptionist to the Cashier page. From there they must find the patient again.
  • The amount is a single total. The desk cannot see which visits or services it is made of, so cannot tell the patient what the 2,000 birr is for.
  • Old debt is only collectable for a patient who has already been received (the "Collect ETB … still to pay" box). For a patient being received now, the old debt is deliberately kept out of today's payment, and there is no way to add it.

What it should do

Abebe Kebede P000033 · 29y male · 0916 … TODAY Registration / visit fee No charge. Registered 26 Aug 2026 (Nehase 20, 2018 EC). One time only. Consultation 300.00 EARLIER UNPAID 2 bills · 2,000.00 · oldest 38 days [Select all] [ ] 3 Sep Root canal, visit 2 unpaid at the cashier 1,200.00 [ ] 12 Sep Extraction UR8 agreed to pay later 800.00 Today 300.00 Earlier bills selected 0.00 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── To pay now 300.00 [ Collect 300.00 & send through ]

Ticking a bill adds it to the same payment. One receipt covers everything.

How it works, step by step

  1. The receptionist opens the patient. The panel lists each unpaid bill: date, what it was for, amount, and whether it is waiting at the cashier or was agreed to be paid later.
  2. The desk says it aloud: "Abebe, you have two unpaid bills, 2,000 birr in total, from 3 and 12 September."
  3. If the patient agrees to pay now, the receptionist ticks one bill, both, or uses Select all. The total at the bottom updates.
  4. One payment method, one receipt, one press of Collect. If the patient will pay only part, they choose which bills to settle.
  5. Nothing is ticked by default, so a patient is never charged for old debt they did not agree to pay.

Rules I would build in

  • Bank transfers stay honest. With the fix for issue 6 (done), a transfer the bank confirmed as 2,300 can pay up to 2,300 and no more. If the patient pays 300 by transfer and ticks 2,000 of old debt, the desk is told exactly that and unticks, or pays the old bills another way.
  • Only people who may collect money see tick boxes. A receptionist without the cashier permission still sees the list and the total (so they can tell the patient), but is told the bills are at the cashier.
  • Unbilled treatment is shown separately. Dental treatment the dentist has not yet sent to the cashier is not a bill yet. It shows as "Not yet billed · 1,500" with the existing Open to bill it link.
  • Pay later stays a separate decision and does not touch old debt. Today's "Pay later…" on this panel also defers old debt (a known problem, see section 3), which would conflict with this feature.
  • Every bill is still recorded with who took it and when, as now.

Effort and what it needs

Medium About a day. The server can already settle an exact list of bills in one receipt (the same call reception uses today for today's charges), and already lists a patient's bills. The work is mostly the panel, plus tests: ticking, totals, the bank-amount refusal, a desk without collect rights, a patient with nothing owed.

Not included: paying part of a single bill (for example 500 of a 1,200-birr bill). The system settles whole bills. It is tracked as FIN-8 and would be its own piece of work.

F. Behind the scenes

  • The page asks the server a heavy question every 30 seconds, per open screen. The three lists each recompute the clinic's whole treatment-money picture (the same one Finance uses) and then keep only today's people. With 600 patients it is fast; it grows with every patient and every treatment. Small First measure it with real clinic data; then limit the calculation to the people being shown.
  • Updates by polling. A patient finished by the dentist appears at reception up to 30 seconds late. A live feed already exists for the queue; reception could use it. Medium
  • The panel makes a handful of separate requests when a patient is opened (settings, departments, clinicians, services, payment methods, registration status). Most are cached after the first patient. One request that returns everything the panel needs would be tidier but is not urgent. Small

4. Decisions for you

  1. Old bills on the receptionist's screen: may a receptionist without the cashier permission see the itemised list (to tell the patient), or only a total? My recommendation: see the list, no tick boxes.
  2. Old bills ticked by default? My recommendation: none ticked, with a Select all button.
  3. Paying part of an old bill (an amount, not a whole bill): worth building now, or later? My recommendation: later; choosing whole bills covers most cases.
  4. Usual dentist as the default: pre-select the dentist the patient saw last time? My recommendation: yes, always changeable.
  5. Emergency: who may mark a patient as an emergency, any receptionist or only with a reason? My recommendation: any receptionist; it is recorded.
  6. Receipt by SMS: use the existing patient SMS (it has a cost per message), or print only? My recommendation: print by default, SMS on request.

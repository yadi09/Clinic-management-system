## Cross-Cutting Engineering Observations (derived strictly from screen names)

- **Auth modes** explicitly named on the single Login screen: workspace, SSO, MFA.
- **Impersonation** appears as a first-class platform capability in two places: Super Admin #16 (Impersonation) and Support #24 (Active support session — impersonation). Implies audited cross-tenant session-assumption with corresponding entries in the Audit log screens.
- **Audit log** is a screen for SaaS Owner (3.2 #11), Super Admin (3.2 #19), and Tenant Owner (3.3 #13) — three role-scoped views of audit data.
- **Feature flags** appear at both tiers: platform-level SaaS Owner (3.2 #07) + Super Admin "Flag overrides" (3.2 #17), and tenant-level Tenant Owner (3.3 #08). Implies a hierarchical flag system with platform defaults overridable per-tenant.
- **Queue surfaces** are role-differentiated: Branch Manager "Queue monitor" (3.3 #19), Nurse "Triage / queue" (3.3 #31), Receptionist "Queue board" (3.3 #41), Lab Tech "Worklist" / Lab Manager "Worklist (all)" (3.4 #01/#08), Pharmacist "Dispense queue" (3.4 #11).
- **Billing/finance is two-layered:** platform billing (3.2 #26–30: Revenue, Subscriptions, Invoices, Payments & dunning, Reports & exports) vs tenant finance (Cashier 3.4 #27–32 and Accountant 3.4 #33–40). "Subscription & usage" also surfaces at tenant level (Tenant Owner 3.3 #11).
- **Repeated/overlapping screen names** (likely shared components rendered with different role scopes/data): Departments (TO #05 / BM #17); Patient search (Doctor #24 / Receptionist #37); Purchasing (Pharmacy Mgr #18 / Inventory Mgr #25); Price lists & discounts (TO 3.3 #09 / Accountant 3.4 #38); Reports/Financial reports/Branch reports appearing across TO, BM, Pharmacy Mgr, Accountant, HR.
- **EMR / clinical workflow chain** (Doctor): Consultation & EMR workspace #25 → Prescription #26 / Lab orders #27 / Dynamic form #28 → Results review #29 → Referrals & follow-ups #30. "Dynamic form" implies a configurable form engine.
- **Lab pipeline state implied by screen ordering:** Accession sample → Result entry → (Lab Manager) Validation queue → Critical values / Turnaround & QA — a draft→entered→validated result lifecycle, though no explicit enum/state-machine values are given in the doc.

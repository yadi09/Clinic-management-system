ClinicOS CRM — build specification

The prototype's logic and interface, on the production foundation · 25 September 2026

The prototype is the design; ClinicOS is the engine. Its feature logic — a lead score with published weights, ten saved views, a real task queue, a funnel and a leaderboard — is better thought through than what ClinicOS has today, and its interface is better. Build that interface and that logic, on top of the Postgres schema, real auth and duplicate detection that already exist. This document is the spec for doing so.

01What the prototype gets right

Read out of the running app and its bundle, not guessed at.

The lead score — the best idea in it

A published 0–100 score built from three different kinds of signal, with every weight visible to the user in Settings:

Contact completenessClinic qualityPipeline readiness
+15 primary phone valid
+15 doctor contact named
+10 WhatsApp connected		 +10 Google rating > 4.0
+15 multi-chair clinic (3+)		 +25 demo scheduled
+30 active trial / pilot
+35 negotiation stage

Why it works: it is explainable. A rep can see why a clinic scores 70 and what would move it. It rewards doing the work (name the doctor, get the WhatsApp number) as well as the clinic's own attractiveness. And publishing the weights means nobody argues with the number — they argue about the weights, which is the right argument to have.

Note what this implies: the prototype tracks chair count and WhatsApp reachability per clinic. Neither exists in ClinicOS, and chair count is the same axis we discussed metering the subscription plans on.

Ten saved views

All Clinics · My Leads · Hot Leads · Today's Follow-ups · Overdue · Uncontacted · Demos · Trials · Won · Lost

One tap each. This is the difference between a database and a tool: a rep never constructs a query, they pick the question they already had. Uncontacted and Overdue in particular are the two that stop leads rotting.

The lead row

Eleven columns, each earning its place:

Clinic name + ★rating (reviews) │ Subcity + landmark │ Phone + email
Doctor + role │ Stage │ Priority │ Rep │ Last contact │ Next action (OVERDUE)
Score │ Quick action

The Google rating beside the name is the detail I would not have thought of: leads are sourced from Google Maps, so the rating and review count are free, and "4.8 with 104 reviews" is a different prospect from "3.8 with 12". Qualification signal at zero cost.

The rest of the logic worth keeping

Follow-up queueOverdue / Today / Upcoming / Completed, with +1 Day and +3 Days snooze on every row. Snooze is what stops a queue becoming a wall of guilt — 68 overdue is survivable if you can push three of them.

02What ClinicOS brings that the prototype cannot

  • tenant_id on a prospect — the join from "clinic we are courting" to "clinic running ClinicOS". The sale closes into a workspace. A localStorage app has nothing to close into.
  • Duplicate detection — four reps scraping Google Maps will collect the same clinic twice.
  • Configurable phases per campaign — better than eleven hardcoded stages.
  • Real accounts — argon2, MFA, sessions, the reset-link flow. The prototype prints its password on the login page.
  • Postgres, RLS, audit — and 7 real prospects rather than 100 invented ones.

One new table

crm_tasks(id, prospect_id, title, kind, due_at, done_at,
          owner_id, note, snoozed_from, created_by, created_at)

Because next_action_at is one date on one prospect, and a rep needs several tasks against one clinic, a queue to work down, and a snooze that records it was pushed rather than silently moving the date.

04The scoring engine, specified

score = min(100,
    15 × (phone is a valid Ethiopian mobile)      // kernel/phone already decides this
  + 15 × (contact_name is set AND contact_role is set)
  + 10 × (whatsapp is set)
  + 10 × (rating > 4.0)
  + 15 × (chairs >= 3)
  + 25 × (reached "Demo Scheduled" or beyond)
  + 30 × (reached "Trial" or beyond)
  + 35 × (reached "Negotiation" or beyond))

Computed server-side on write, stored on the row, shown with its breakdown on hover so a rep can see why. Weights live in one Go table to begin with, exposed in the console later — the prototype shows them read-only, which is the right first step.

One correction to the prototype's maths. Its weights sum to 155 and the stage bonuses stack, so anything in negotiation is pinned at 100 and the score stops discriminating exactly where it matters most. Make the stage component the highest reached, not the sum — 35 for negotiation, not 25+30+35 — so the top of the list still sorts meaningfully.

06Interface

Both are React + Tailwind, so this is a re-skin rather than a rewrite. Every prototype pattern maps to a component ClinicOS already owns:

PrototypeBuilt from
Sidebar with live counts and an OVERDUE badgeConsole nav + the Segmented badge pattern from Reception
Saved-view tab stripSegmented — same component as Chairside's lenses
Stage / priority pillsBadge variants
Dense sortable tableTable, as on the Staff page
Lead drawerExisting ProspectDialog, widened
Keep the console's dark palette. The prototype is light-on-white; the platform console is dark. Take the structure — sidebar, view tabs, filter row, dense table, pills — and not the colours. Copying both is how you end up with something that looks like two products stitched together.

07Plan

Phase 1 — The listno migration~1 session
Dense table · 10 saved views · filters · sort · pagination · quick-log. ListProspects already filters status, owner and priority, so this is mostly frontend. Highest value per hour on the list.
Phase 2 — Follow-upscrm_tasks~1–2 sessions
The queue, four tabs, snooze, add task. This is what a rep opens every morning.
Phase 3 — Lead quality6 columns~1 session
subcity · rating · reviews · chairs · whatsapp · score, plus the scoring engine and the subcity filter. Sorting by score is the moment the list starts working for you.
Phase 4 — Analyticsqueries only~1 session
Funnel from crm_phase_events, leaderboard, subcity density, source ROI. No new tables — the data is already recorded, it has just never been asked for.
Phase 5 — Excel import~1 session
Column mapping, dry run, duplicate check against /prospects/duplicates. Reuse the patient CSV importer — that work is done and proven.
Phase 6 — Calendar and polish~1 session
Month view over tasks, channel filters, prospects in the Ctrl-K palette.

08Not doing

  • Not migrating the 100 fabricated clinics — they would pollute a pipeline holding 7 real ones.
  • Not hardcoding 11 stages — seed them as one campaign's phases and expect to cut to six. Four reps will not keep eleven columns honest.
  • Not rebuilding auth, invites or the RBAC matrix — ClinicOS is far ahead there; the prototype's version is a drawing of one.
  • Not building Clear Database / Reload Sample Data — coherent in a toy, a loaded gun on real data.
  • Not starting before the backups exist. Adding tables to an unbacked database increases what there is to lose.

09Decide before phase 1

  1. Stages — all 11, or New → Contacted → Interested → Demo → Trial → Negotiation → Won/Lost?
  2. Where it lives — stay inside the platform console, or its own app at /crm? The prototype's full-screen sidebar implies the latter, and it is the better home if reps who are not vendor admins will use it.
  3. Scope — all six phases, or stop after three and see whether the reps use it?
